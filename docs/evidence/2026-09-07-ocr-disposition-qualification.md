## Qualified behavior and limits

[Same-call diagnostics](../ocr-detection-disposition.md) follow actual returned
detections through crop/classification/recognition, blank and score removal,
retained output and unresolved outcomes across page, region and hard-scan
retries. Raw dispatch completion, observed method/model outcomes, candidate
acceptance and physical mapping remain separate. Ordinal identities are not
inferred by matching a second detector run, duplicate text or nearby boxes.
Unavailable stages are not fabricated as zero detections. Detector-score
correspondence is unavailable where upstream sorting does not preserve it.

The optional observer reserves its dispatch ticket and full-cohort fallback
before OCR. Ordinary diagnostic faults make details unavailable without
discarding healthy candidates; actual model/guard failures and cancellation
remain failures. Exact binary32 replay and bounded source mapping retain
unavailable geometry explicitly. Consumed work is not refunded by fallback.
The fixed characterized recipe observes existing settings rather than silently
changing unsupported ones. Selected work/allocation bounds do not guarantee
total process memory or complete source capture.

Private create-only bundles publish the exact legacy report, existing execution
receipt and new strict companion before their manifest. Fresh readback binds
the independently supplied current request and producer generation. The CLI
supervises one fixed worker, supports verification without OCR, and retains
partial artifacts after interruption. Existing candidate/report/receipt
schemas, defaults, canonical extraction, indexes and AI permissions are
unchanged. No models, packages or private reference corpus were acquired.

Focused model-free scopes passed: 392 leaf/geometry/observer/runtime controls,
142 bundle/CLI controls, 623 legacy guard/receipt/stage/reader controls and a
389-test integration review scope. These overlap and must not be added.
The full suite also includes installed-source characterization with inert
sessions. Controls cover honest partial upstream failures, missing-ticket
refusal before dispatch, diagnostic-only faults, bounded fallback and healthy
peers after failed attempts. Those controls are not native failure-path proof.
